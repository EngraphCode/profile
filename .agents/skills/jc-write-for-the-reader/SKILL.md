description: "The order of work for drafting or revising any copy that represents a person (a LinkedIn profile's fields, a CV, a front page, a bio, a post) or prose for an external reader's decision (a letter, a bio for a third party), with the author present and the author the authority on every fact and every sentence: the reader's decision on paper first, the audience hops the words will cross, the two or three beliefs the piece must leave, the moment that proves each, continuous prose in one voice, the facts verified afterwards, the readers (the voice check; editor, then prose-expert; the audience-reader on the revised draft), then the author. Load it whenever asked to write, draft, redraft, tighten, edit or \"make this better\" for such a surface, even for one field or one paragraph. Not for records, plans, pull-request text, commit messages, code comments or Practice prose, which have their own conventions. Right looks like: the reader's decision shown with the draft, one job per paragraph, every sentence changing what the reader believes, every proposal a sentence the author can accept or edit. Wrong looks like: a label, a colon and a litany; a draft assembled from the sources' fragments; the author's chat answer pasted as copy; copy cut to a character count; a card partitioned by a date seam."
