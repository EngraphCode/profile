# EDR-009: The Oak Principal card under LinkedIn's limits — one piece of writing, the how in its Project cards, never a partition by date

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-21

## Context

LinkedIn's field limits were verified at the live edit forms by Jim, reading each field's own
counter: the headline 220, the About 2,600 and a position's description (the field LinkedIn now
labels Highlights) 2,000 on 19 October 2026; a project's description 2,000 on 21 October. The
validator's structure table and the apply skill's field-limits reference carry the figures. The
merged Principal Engineer card, his writing of 19 October, measured 2,852 characters, the only
field over its limit. Four options were put to him on 19 October: verify the limit, move depth
to the existing Project cards, two positions under Oak, or a recraft in one card; nothing was to
be cut.

On 21 October he asked whether two Principal positions would be a simple split or a rewrite. The
seat counted the paragraphs by period and answered a split with one seam, four of six paragraphs
moving whole. He asked for a diff page in the editor and the split shown on it. The seat built
the page, then wrote the split into the review file: two positions, January 2021 to May 2022 and
June 2022 to the present, his paragraphs sorted into the period they belonged to, and sentences
written to join them where a paragraph crossed the seam. When the later card came out 28
characters over, the sentence on joining permanently moved to the earlier card. When he said
important language had gone and the early interventions were in the wrong period (the first QA
engineer, the first engineering manager and the first platform group came early; the design of
the team structure came later), the growth sentences were cut again along the same seam.

His verdict, verbatim: "you are turning it from good writing into a litany of facts with no
soul and no connection". He sent the seat to the editorial skills and directives to reflect and
report, changing nothing.

The reflection, agreed with him. The split was written for the material, two date ranges and a
count, and the material's shape leaked into the prose, which is the failure the
write-for-the-reader skill names in its first section. The joining sentences were labels and
tells ("The decision that shaped this period was X. It followed Y."), standing where his story
had been. His sentences whose meaning is their span ("Two decisions shaped the last four years";
"At each scale I made the interventions with the most impact: early ..., later ...") were halved,
and a half of a span carries nothing. The count chose where the seam fell, so "nothing cut" held
for his words and failed for the structure. The public-copy rule did not fire because the seat
called the seam "two sentences". And the diff page, built that morning, was the instrument the
seat judged the copy with: a diff shows what moved and what changed, never whether the result
reads as a person. It was the fourth instance in October 2026 of the shape that skill was written
against: a label, a colon and a litany; a draft assembled from settled pieces; copy shaped to a
number.

His card is thematic: one job per paragraph, responsibility, the two decisions, the growth and
the interventions at each scale, the choice to stay, the rebuild, the plugin, the Practice, the
pattern. Its joints are between themes, not between years. Cutting along time forces every
spanning paragraph to be duplicated, halved or glued; cutting along the joints keeps the writing.

## Decision

1. **Two Oak cards.** The Senior Developer card (August to December 2020) as it stands, and the
   Principal Engineer card from January 2021 to the present as one piece of writing. No card is
   partitioned by date to meet a limit.
2. **The Principal card carries what only an Experience card can say**: the responsibility and
   how it is carried; the two decisions and their organising principles; the organisation growing
   and the intervention made at each scale; the choice to stay; and for the rebuild and the
   plugin, the why, the call, the cost accepted, the authority behind it and what it changed (the
   teachers' hours, the reach, what stands on it). Decision, cost, consequence, authority, impact.
3. **The Project cards carry the how.** The Oak AI Plugin, the Practice and the rebuild each have
   a Project card under a limit of 2,000. They take the mechanism: the staged adoption, public
   site then teacher then pupil; the SDK, semantic search, the MCP app and where the plugin is
   available; the Practice's working, the human defining vision and strategy while agents build.
   They are warrants a reader opens wanting the particulars, written for that reader by the
   method, never cut from the card and pasted.
4. **Featured is a showcase, never a home for copy.** It takes a post, an article, a link, an
   image or a document, each with a title and a line. The three links stand (the plugin page, the
   Claude connector, Oak's site).
5. **The FT Web App stays a Project card**, the earliest beside the Oak three, reworded in the
   past tense (its opening is a label without a verb); its awards carry "lauded at the time";
   whether it names his part in building it is his to say.
6. **The method binds every card and every seam.** The write-for-the-reader order from its first
   section: the reader's decision on paper and shown with the draft, the beliefs and the moments,
   continuous prose in his voice, verification, the voice check, `editor`, `prose-expert`, the
   `audience-reader`, dispositions, then Jim. A sentence written to join two of his is copy and
   fires the method. A diff is read for what moved, never for whether it reads. A count is read
   once, as information; if a card written by the method still refuses its field, what moves to
   another surface is editorial and his.
7. **The fallback shape, if he chooses it.** Two positions under Oak by employment type, contract
   then permanent, as his draft of 12 October had, is a legitimate LinkedIn shape with 4,000
   characters between them. If chosen, each position is its own piece of writing from its own
   reader's decision, never a partition of the merged card.

## Consequences

- The split in the review file is withdrawn; the review file is the source again, and the
  editor's diff page shows no difference until the next proposal or his next edit.
- The next act on the lane, in order: the reader's decision and the beliefs for the Principal
  card on paper, before any draft; one draft by the method; the three Project cards and the FT
  card; the readers with dispositions; then Jim. The count is measured once and stated.
- The write-for-the-reader skill carries this instance in its gotchas, so the shape is named at
  drafting time and not only here.
- The diff page stays, as the surface for reading his edits against the source and a proposal
  against the source, with the caveat in decision 6.

## Alternatives considered

- **Two Principal positions by date, partitioned from the merged card.** Rejected for the reason
  in the context: the arc is the writing, and a seam through it leaves halves and labels. Kept
  only as the fallback shape of decision 7, written fresh.
- **One card recrafted to 2,000 by cutting.** Rejected: copy is never cut to a count (the
  public-copy rule, his words of 8 October).
- **The how moved into Featured.** Rejected: Featured's structure (decision 4) takes no copy.
