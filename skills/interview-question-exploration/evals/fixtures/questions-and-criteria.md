# Head of Digital Service, Ashcombe Borough Council (invented)

Everything in this file is invented for testing the skill. The organisation, the role, the
criteria and the questions describe no real employer.

## The role

Head of Digital Service in the council's housing and benefits directorate. Leads a team of
twenty-two (engineers, designers, a data analyst, product managers) running the online services
residents use to report repairs, apply for benefits and pay rent. Reports to the Director of
Resident Services. Salary band G. Applications are sifted anonymously on a platform that hides
the applicant's name and CV from the people scoring.

## Published criteria

Essential:

1. Leading a multidisciplinary digital team through change, with evidence of decisions that
   shaped the team.
2. Running live public-facing services to a standard: availability, accessibility and data
   protection.
3. Prioritising within a fixed and reducing budget, and explaining the choices to the people
   affected.
4. Improving how a team works, with evidence that the improvement lasted.
5. Learning from service failures, and changing the service rather than the explanation.
6. Working with frontline staff and residents as sources of evidence for decisions.
7. Developing people: coaching, delegation and succession.

Desirable:

8. Working with suppliers and other councils on shared services.

## The council's note to applicants on help with your answers

You may get help with drafting, from a person or from a tool. The examples must be yours and the
words must be your own. We mark down answers that could have been written by anyone, or by a
machine, and we will ask you about your answers at interview.

## The sift questions

Each is answered in 250 words or fewer and scored on its own, one to five, by the person who
wrote it.

**Q1.** Tell us about a time you stopped or redirected a piece of work your team had already
invested in. How did you reach the decision, how did you take the people who had built it with
you, and what did the freed capacity do?

**Q2.** Tell us about a service you were responsible for failed the people who depend on it.
What did you do in the first days, what did you change afterwards, and what of that change is
still in place?

**Q3.** Tell us about introducing a new way of working to a team that did not want it. What
was the resistance, how did you work with it, and what is the practice's state now?

**Q4.** Tell us about a time a budget or headcount reduction landed on your area. What did you
choose to protect, what did you drop, and how did you explain that to the people it affected?
