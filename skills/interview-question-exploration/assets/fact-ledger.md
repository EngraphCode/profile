# Fact ledger

Every particular that appears in any answer has a row here before it is drafted. Nothing goes
into an answer that is not here as confirmed or checkable. This document is private to the
writer and is never submitted; it is also the interview preparation.

Status: `confirmed` (the writer confirms it from memory or their own records), `checkable` (a
named document or public page holds it), `unconfirmed` (stays out of the answers until it moves).

Attribution class, for claims about what the writer did: `decided`, `influenced`, `contributed`,
`observed`. See `references/truth-and-attribution.md`.
