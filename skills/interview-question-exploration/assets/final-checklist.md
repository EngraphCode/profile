# Final checklist

Run through this for every answer before pasting it into the form, and once more for the set.

## Each answer

- [ ] Every part of the question is answered, in the order asked.
- [ ] The first sentence is the particular: where, when, what was at stake, at what scale.
- [ ] My decision or act is in the first person with a verb that matches the ledger's class.
- [ ] The other people in the question appear as people I read and responded to: what they
      needed, said or resisted, and what I did about it.
- [ ] Where the question asked how, the method is named and the reason I chose it is there.
- [ ] For a technical question, the reasoning is technical and named, not preference or rank.
- [ ] The cost is honest; for an obstacles question, each obstacle is navigated, not listed.
- [ ] The outcome is a checkable fact of the kind this criterion counts, in its published or
      measured form.
- [ ] There is one honest judgement about this episode (what worked, what I would refine, when I
      let go), specific enough to be wrong, and it is not a moral.
- [ ] The last sentence or two is what I changed so it lasts: an act with a trace, not a belief
      about myself.
- [ ] Every product, team, system and acronym is defined on first use in this answer.
- [ ] No sentence explains why I am the kind of person who would do this.
- [ ] No tell sentence ("this demonstrates"), no moral, no restated outcome at the end.
- [ ] No hedge on a true claim; attribution sits beside each claim instead.
- [ ] At most one polished line; the rest is evidence.
- [ ] Nothing is in the answer that is not in the ledger as confirmed or checkable.
- [ ] I can go two levels deeper on every sentence at interview.
- [ ] I have read it aloud and would say every sentence to a colleague who was there.
- [ ] The assessor read's lever has been worked or consciously declined.
- [ ] It is within the limit as the form counts it (words or characters, spaces as the form
      counts them).

## The set

- [ ] No sentence, list or opener is reused across answers.
- [ ] No episode does duty for more than one question unless there was no alternative, and then
      the two answers use different parts of it.
- [ ] The script ran across all answers together with no hard failures.
- [ ] The employer's statement on assistance has been followed, and I can answer truthfully if
      asked.
- [ ] A dated copy of the final answers and the ledger is kept where I will find it before the
      interview.
