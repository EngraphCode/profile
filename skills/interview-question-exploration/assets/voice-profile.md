## The check

1. Does this sound like [writer], as the lines above sound?
2. Would [writer] say every sentence aloud to a colleague who was there?
3. Is every sentence showing or claiming, never explaining or justifying?
4. Would a reader of all four answers hear one person?
5. Do the measured figures for the finished answers sit inside the writer's range, and where
   they do not, was it a choice?
