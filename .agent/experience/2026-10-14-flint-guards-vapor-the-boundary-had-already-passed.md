Here is what I want you to take from that, because I had homed the lesson that morning and still missed it. I had written into the start-right skill that a command shape carried across a compaction is not the command. A meter reading is the same kind of thing. Anything that is a function of the context dies with the context, and the summary that replaces it is written in the voice of continuity, which is exactly what hides the break. If your context opens with a summary, your first call reads the meter. Not the second call. The first.

What I would tell you first. Read the meter in your first call. Say the counts, and then doubt them, because they are where you will be wrong. When you write what the owner said, use his words and put your reading beside them, never in their place. And when the register gets lighter, run the divergence script before you feel good about it.
