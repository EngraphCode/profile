# LinkedIn industry taxonomy, the rows that matter here

The profile's industry is chosen from LinkedIn's industry taxonomy, version 2. The full table is
public: Microsoft Learn, LinkedIn API reference tables, "Industry Codes V2" (industry ID, label,
hierarchy path, description; active and inactive nodes; LinkedIn notes it is not accepting
additions). Read on 2026-10-11. The profile's picker is believed to use this taxonomy; confirm
at the picker.

The top card in `linkedin/profile.md` says the industry is the sector Oak sits in, chosen at
application. Oak National Academy's own company page shows its industry; read it live and
decide with Jim. The candidate rows:
