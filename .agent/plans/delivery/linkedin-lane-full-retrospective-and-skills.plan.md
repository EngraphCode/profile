## Goal

A record a person running a lane like this one (a profile or a CV for someone, with agents) can
read and so avoid the cost this lane paid; the owner's own account of what he spent and what he
would change, in his words where he writes them, the public part under the second cut and the
rest in the private repository; every lesson of the arc in a named home (a skill, a rule, a Core
record, this node, or discarded with the reason), the partial record extended rather than
duplicated. And the four editorial skills usable by a person who has only the skill text and
their own author file: what a skill now points at inside this repository is carried in the skill,
named as the one input the person supplies, or dropped.

## Decisions that are his, each with the seat's proposal

1. **What the retrospective is for.** Three readers are possible: himself, a future seat on this
   estate, a person outside it. Proposal: one record sectioned by reader, the outsider's section
   first since it is the hardest to write honestly; his account in his words.
2. **The owner's turns as evidence.** Proposal: the seat reads the ledgers whole in the private
   repository and cites turns by date and kind; a quotation appears on the public record only
   where his words are process words (a correction of method), never where they are about him.
3. **Self-sufficiency and its packaging.** His word of 25 October was "no distribution yet";
   his question of 27 October asks whether the skills would be useful to another person.
   Proposal: the skills are made self-contained in text (the general principles they cite from the
   directives carried in the skill; the author file named as the one input; the reader templates
   named as optional readers a person may run or skip), proven by a dry read in which an agent
   given only the skill text and a fictional author's file runs the method and reports what it
   lacked; no bundle, export or publication is built, and whether to publish is his to decide once
   the dry read passes.
4. **What the retrospective's proposals may edit in this lane.** Proposal: the four skills and
   the two editorial directives (under the context rule) in the lane; a rule or a Core clause is a
   proposal to him with its warrant and falsifier, under `new-rule-vs-pdr-clause`.
5. **The sitting shape.** His word of 27 October names the cram. Proposal: two sittings and two
   pull requests, the retrospective record with its landed proposals in one, the skills' self-
   sufficiency with its dry read in the other; neither sitting starts the next.

## Mechanism

- The `retrospective` skill is the method: the causal stack extended to the whole arc, the
  extraction lane's cost included (this sitting ran from 11:51Z to 14:37Z on a bounded brief:
  five reviewers, one cure round, two re-reads, two public review rounds); the counterfactual
  re-run against the cured method; honest credit; every proposal with a warrant and a falsifier,
  routed through the PDR-130 lanes; the disposition table carried forward from the partial record
  with its thirty rows kept and the new rows added.
- His account is taken in conversation, his words verbatim, sorted at writing under the second cut
  (`privacy.md` §Private editorial material); the private half goes to the private author file
  or a sibling document there.
- The self-sufficiency pass: for each skill, every pointer into this repository listed; each
  classified carry, name-as-input or drop; the skill amended; the dry read run by a subagent that
  receives the skill text and a fictional author file only (no repository access) and is asked to
  draft one field and report what it lacked; the gaps cured; the read repeated once. The
  validator `validate-editorial-layers` stays green throughout: no author's name in a general or
  surface file.
- Reviewers, once each: `prose-expert` on the record; `security-expert` on the record for the
  insight class; `docs-adr-expert` with `onboarding-expert` on the skill changes; `test-expert` is
  not dispatched (no test changes). One cure round. The public review's rounds are counted in the
  cost.

## Steps, in order

## Stopping conditions

- The retrospective is complete when the record exists with the extended causal stack, the
  counterfactual, honest credit, his account, and every lesson in a named home.
- The self-sufficiency pass is complete when the second dry read reports nothing lacking that the
  skill text could carry, and the validator is green.
- Each sitting ends at its merge. A finding that would widen the file set beyond the four skills,
  the two directives and the record returns this node to sketch for his word.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The record extends the partial one: every row of the earlier disposition table is present with
   its home, and the new rows carry a home; proof by the seat's read and the owner's.
2. No sentence of the insight class on the public record; proof: `security-expert`'s read, zero
   findings, recorded on the pull request.
3. Each of the four skills carries or names every input its method needs; proof: the dry read's
   report after the cures lists nothing lacking.
4. `validate-editorial-layers` green on both pull requests' tips.
5. Both pull requests merged under his standing rule.
