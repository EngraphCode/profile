# The editorial doctrine as processes for any person; the author's public information in one file; the author's insight in the private repository

Born sketch on 2026-10-11 from the owner's direction for a later time. Taken up on 2026-10-25
when the LinkedIn lane closed and he asked for its understanding extracted in a form that applies
to any person, one session, one pull request. Ratified on 2026-10-26 at the taxonomy in the
frontmatter: guidance, rules and skills are processes and mechanisms; information about a person
is a different sort of thing; the two never share a file or a directory. Amended and re-ratified
on 2026-10-27 at the privacy rule in the frontmatter: information about the author is itself two
sorts of thing, a fact appropriate for the public domain and an insight into the person, and the
second exists only in the private repository. This node is self-contained: a fresh session
implements it from the node, the thread record's current block and the files it names.

## Goal

A seat drafting for any author loads person-neutral guidance (the editorial directives, the
editorial skills, the reader templates) and that author's information, and nothing else. The host
author's public information is one file he can read whole, holding only what he would publish.
His insight, his positioning intent and his working preferences live in the private repository's
author file, which the seat loads when drafting for him and never quotes. No public file in the
repository carries a sentence of the insight class. A check in the gate refuses the author's name
in any guidance file and refuses an author information file inside the doctrine directories.

## User groups and value

- The host author: his public information in one thin file he reads whole, with nothing in it he
  would not put on LinkedIn; his insight in one private file he controls; a correction to either
  never touches process, and a process change never touches his facts. The readers of the public
  repository (the same funders, search partners and leaders his profile addresses) meet only what
  his published surfaces already say.
- A seat drafting public copy for any author: the brief comes from the guidance and the author's
  two files, so a mis-cast reader cannot recur by omission, and the seat knows which sentences it
  may never write into a tracked file.
- Other people, later: the guidance and the surface skills are the body of a distributable skill;
  their own public and private author files follow the model the guidance describes. Packaging is
  not this lane's act (his word of 25 October: distribution "eventually, but not yet").
- The knowledge-graph stream: the author's public editorial facets are sorted into one file with
  a named entity, ready to be expressed as graph when GRAPH-1 reaches them, and nothing of the
  insight class is in the material it would structure.

## The three homes and the two cuts

Every sentence in the editorial scope is sorted by two cuts, in this order.

**First cut: process or information.** A sentence that tells a seat how to write, model, convey,
structure, review or work with an author is process. It lives in a directive, a skill or a reader
template, person-neutral, in one of two layers declared in frontmatter: `layer: general` (the
craft for any surface) or `layer: surface` (what one surface class needs, verified and dated).
A sentence that says something about the author is information and never lives in those files.

**Second cut, on information: public-appropriate or insight.** Information is public-appropriate
when it is a fact or a decision the author has published: it is on the LinkedIn profile, the CV,
the site or an editorial decision record. It lands in the public information file; the voice
adjectives are the one exception, at the owner's word (decision 1). Information is insight when
it describes the
author's inner states, self-view, values as psychology, attitudes, feared readings, an impression
a surface is meant to leave unstated, a positioning rationale, intentions about his career, or
working preferences he has not published. It lands in the private repository's author file.
Insight is always sensitive, whoever wrote the sentence, and a line's presence in the public tree
is never evidence that it is public-appropriate.

**The test runs on prose, not on names.** The sort reads each in-scope file whole as prose, one
sentence at a time, and gives every sentence one home. A search for vocabulary of the insight
class runs afterwards as a check and never stands in for the read. Removing the author's name
from a sentence does not change its home: in this repository "the author" is the owner.

## Mechanism

- **The guidance directive** `.agent/directives/editorial-guidance.md` keeps its name and its
  place in the always-loaded set, because every live referrer wants editorial guidance, and
  becomes that for any author (`layer: general`; `author_names` removed): the author's
  information model (what a public author file holds, what a private author file holds, as the
  template for any person); the conveying principles in general form (show, don't justify;
  agency through outcomes; level shows, never argued; "my" not "the"; breadth as range anchored
  by staying-power evidence; the cumulative impression never stated; a headline filters and the
  first paragraph rewards; collaborative credit; a background grounds the positioning without
  being claimed; a sensibility shown, never claimed; a frame claim honours delivery; a quality
  shown is earned on the page; keywords carried by structured data); the direction of derivation
  (author information and guidance, then the content model, then derived surfaces) and
  consistency across outputs (ADR-011); the decision-record mechanism; working with the author in
  brief, pointing at `write-for-the-reader` §Working with the author; and the loading rule: a
  seat drafting for an author loads the public author file and, where the host has one, the
  private author file outside the public tree, and quotes neither on a public surface. One
  host-binding paragraph points at this host's public author file and at `privacy.md`.
- **The composition directive** `.agent/directives/editorial-strategy.md` stays general
  (`layer: general`): its five mentions of the author's name become "the author"; §Standing
  audiences stays as the generic audience list of any professional surface with EDR-008's
  decision axis, in neutral words; the Authority table's row for the guidance directive is
  reworded.
- **The public information file** `docs/editorial/author.md`, titled with his name, frontmatter
  `author_names: [Jim, Cresswell, jimcresswell]` and `entity: https://www.jimcresswell.net/#person`,
  no `layer`, no fitness keys: identity in one line and the entity; the voice adjectives as a
  presentation statement; the registers' shared topics; positioning vocabulary with his published
  meanings (impact as climate, ecological and societal good; zero to one; product safety and the
  sense of "Responsible AI"; "helping build"); the research facts and the two lines never
  conflated; the Oak SDK and MCP fact; the standing decisions as one-line pointers to their

  headline); the Skills pins and tiers of 11 October 2026 as the record of origin of the list in

  Formatted by the repository's prettier and markdownlint.

  the ignored nested repository under the directory `privacy.md` §Private editorial material

  request for him to merge. Nothing about its content is written in a public file beyond this
  paragraph's description of kinds.
- **Skills and templates.** `write-for-the-reader` and `editorial-voice` (general),
  `linkedin-profile` and `cv` (surface), and the reader templates `editor`, `prose-expert` and

## The disposition of the guidance directive

The input is every sentence of `.agent/directives/editorial-guidance.md` at commit dc1e5e58
(244 lines). Each sentence has exactly one home: the guidance directive in general form, the
public author file, or the private author file. The ledger that records the sentence, its home
and its status (`pending`, then `applied`) is instance data about the author, because its private
rows index the insight class, so it lives in the private repository beside the private author
file, at the place its README assigns. The ledger's reader is the implementer at the step that
writes the three files and the owner at his read of both pull requests; the decision it changes is
which file each sentence lands in. The public node records only the destinations (the Mechanism
section lists both public destinations' contents in full), the count of sentences, and the
stopping condition that every row of the private ledger is `applied`.
