# What the LinkedIn lane can teach, and how to extract it

Exploration notes, 2026-10-25, Shrew rides Eventide (371a2b), at the owner's word on the day the
live profile was finished: "I would like to extract whatever insight we can into editorial best
practices, writing best practices, linkedin best practices and approaches, cv writing best
practices and approaches, and any other editorial or professional profile or adjacent field
knowledge. Please use all appropriate skills to explore how we might do this ... Explore, and make
notes, but do not start the actual retro yet", then "step one, please use all appropriate skills
to improve my request, then action it".

Skills applied: `metacognition` (generative mode, the request read as a pointer wider than its
words), `reason` (the kind named, the problem framed, the stop gate run), `proportionality` (the
size, the instrument, the level), `concept-exploration` (the four movements below),
`free-play` (a bounded harvest, §7), `wrap` (its loss scan as an instrument over this seat's
context, §8, without the close), `retrospective` (read for the instrument's shape and its
warning about whole-corpus reading; not run), `plan` (the design gate only; no node authored).
Nothing here is a decision; §9 names the decisions, which are the owner's.

## 1. The request, improved

The request as given names five kinds of knowledge and no reader, no finish and no reading
method. The improved request:

```text
From the LinkedIn lane of 26 September to 25 October 2026, extract the knowledge the next
piece of writing about a person would otherwise relearn, and land each piece where its reader
will meet it: the method of writing a professional profile with its subject present (the
editorial directives, the editorial skills and the editorial decision records), the platform
facts, dated (the apply skill's references), and, if the owner wants one, a person-neutral
form of the method (a distributable skill beside interview-question-exploration), the three landing in the three-layer shape he
sketched on 11 October if he ratifies it. Read the owner's corrections first, extracted
mechanically from the sessions' user turns, because they are the external signal the seat's
own records lack; read the seat's buffers, the comms events, the napkins, the plans and the
workspace notes second; read nothing whole. Keep his reasons and the private brief out of
tracked files. The retrospective
on the arc's cost and shape is a separate act at his word and reads the same ledger. Finish:
every candidate insight has a home or a reasoned discard, the next editorial lane (the CV)
starts from the landed method, and the ledger is ready for the retrospective.
```

What changed and why:

- **A reader for every piece.** "Best practices" without a reader is the litany this lane learnt
  to refuse: generic guidance already exists in the September research (two reports, 116 KB).
  What the research could not hold is what the work taught: how to write a real person's
  profile with that person, which failures recur, and what the platform does with the text.
  Each piece lands where its reader loads it, or it is not landed.
- **A finish.** "Whatever insight we can" is unbounded; the owner's standing word is that work
  needs criteria, sizing and ratified value, and a goal understood well enough to finish. The
  finish above is checkable.
- **Two acts, one reading.** The retrospective asks why the arc cost what it cost and how it
  unfolded (a causal stack, a counterfactual, honest credit, proposals). The extraction asks
  what the lane knows about writing a profile. Different kinds, different homes, one corpus:
  read it once, route to both, and the retrospective waits for his word.
- **The sieve at the front.** His reasons are private and his decisions public (his word of 11
  October); the application brief is owner-private; records on public surfaces carry no
  swearing. The extraction is designed with the sieve first, not applied after a draft exists.
- **A reading method sized to the corpus.** 812 MB of transcripts is not read whole; the last
  whole-corpus retrospective in this estate met his correction for exactly that. The method in
  §5 reads the small, high-signal stratum first and holds the heavy instrument in reserve.

## 3. Movement one: the observations

1. **Much was consolidated at occurrence.** `write-for-the-reader` was written from three
   failures of one week and carries his corrections of 7, 8, 10 and 21 October verbatim; EDR-008
   and EDR-009 hold the two shape decisions; `apply-linkedin-profile` holds the platform facts
   of 24 and 25 October as dated gotchas; `linkedin-skills` holds the selection method and a
   registry with provenance per name. The extraction is a gap read against these, not a first
   capture.
2. **What lives only in his words.** The corrections that changed the method arrived in chat,
   and the seat captured each in its own words in the per-user buffer and the thread record.
   The buffer is not in the repository and is loaded by one seat; the thread record is history
   by the time the next lane opens. His own sentences, in order, with what the seat had just
   done, are in the transcripts and nowhere else.
3. **The seat's recurring error classes, as named during the lane:**
   - reaching for an external authority (a taxonomy, the CV, the graph, a recruiter's frame)
     when the authority was his word and the understanding the headline and About carry (the
     plan node of 11 October names this as the recurring error the method must prevent);
   - carrying words across an audience hop unchanged: his answers written as copy ("I wrote them
     for you, I did not write them for the audience of linkedin"), a reviewer's finding applied
     as a cure, the sources' sentences assembled into a draft, a CV sentence moved into a
     LinkedIn field, a settled list's shape leaking into prose;
   - counting as choosing: a card cut four times to a believed limit, then partitioned by a date
     seam to meet a verified one ("a litany of facts with no soul and no connection");
   - treating the platform's acknowledgement as proof: a form-filled tick the server never saw,
     a Save alert over a dropped media link, a card whose two shown skills nothing controls.
4. **The collaboration instruments that held**, each from his word: a table he edits plus a
   numbered reply as his review instrument; one draft at a time, never a menu; proposed copy
   short and at the top of the message; every message leading with what changed that he did not
   ask for; a settled item never relisted; his answers facts, never copy; his reasons private,
   his decisions public; a name he set never proposed for removal; nothing trimmed to a number;
   every live change proved by re-opening the dialog, never by the alert; the live profile
   edited only at his express request with him present.
5. **The platform inventory**, dated: the verified limits (headline 220, About 2,600, a
   position's and a project's description 2,000); what each surface shows collapsed (the About's
   fold, a position card's two skills and a count the seat does not control, a project card's
   first two skills in its dialog's order, the Projects list in date order with undated entries
   last); which dialogs reach which surfaces; which edits notify whom; the PDF export as the
   before-and-after record of a surface with no history of its own.
6. **The research came first and said what; the practice taught how and with whom.** The two
   September reports are at the altitude of what strong profiles communicate. No report holds
   the subject-and-seat protocol in observation 4, and that protocol is where the lane's cost
   went.
7. **The surface's source of truth flipped mid-lane.** The owner edited the live profile in
   parallel (a project added, two skills added, the About amended), and the repository copy
   followed the live surface from then on. For a surface its owner also edits by hand, the
   repository holds a record of the live state, not its source, and a read-back precedes every
   write.
8. **The landing shape already exists as a sketch.** The plan node
   `editorial-doctrine-in-three-layers` (born 11 October from his direction, verbatim in the
   node: "we need to distinguish between general writing and editorial best practice, best
   practice for particular surfaces, such as CVs, linkedin, blogs, long-form essays, and within
   those the particular instance of that class applied to a person as the author") separates
   the editorial files into general practice, the surface class and the author instance, each
   in its own directory, with a validator that refuses a person's name in a general file. His
   request of 25 October names the same three layers in other words: writing practice, LinkedIn
   and CV practice, and the rest. The sketch was "not for right now" while the rewrite was the
   priority; the rewrite is finished, and its gate expires on 1 November.

## 4. Movement two: the problem

- **Kind.** Curation and method extraction: deciding what the lane knows, where each piece
  lands, and by what reading. A complicated system, not a complex one: the material is finite,
  the homes exist, the pipeline exists (capture, distil, graduate; two speeds under PDR-130).
  The altitude is strategy first (what the extraction is for and who reads it), mechanism second
  (how to read the corpus).
- **Gap.** The lane's knowledge sits in five tiers, and the tier that holds the external signal
  (his corrections in sequence) is the one no later seat loads.
- **Who it harms.** The next editorial lanes: the CV (his word of 11 October: the LinkedIn work
  feeds the CV and the graph later), the front page, bios and posts; and any later use of the
  method for another person. Each would relearn the error classes of §3.3 at the owner's
  expense, which is where this lane's cost went.
- **Mechanism.** Consolidation at occurrence captures what the seat noticed. The seat's blind
  spots are, by construction, what the owner's corrections caught; those live in his words.
- **Constraints.** The sieve (his reasons, the brief, the profanity rule); no ceremony and no
  folds until he says otherwise; his attention spent only on named decisions; the whole-corpus
  read's known cost; pull requests counted as the cost before work starts.
- **Success.** The finish in §1.

not; the LinkedIn platform inventory of §3.5 is such a file's content, and the CV's print and
page hierarchy is another's. The decision is the owner's (§9).

## 6. Movement four: the synthesis and the proposals

The organising concept, offered as a candidate and not a finding: **words never cross an
audience hop unchanged.** The lane is a chain of hops (his words to the seat; the seat's draft
to the readers; the readers' beliefs to the seat; the draft to him; the profile to its readers;
the profile to the platform's collapsed forms), and every error class in §3.3 is a carry across
one hop without rewriting for the next audience. The research's altitude (what) and the
practice's (how, with whom) are the same concept at two hops.

## 7. Free play, a bounded harvest

Associations, not findings; each marked as the event it was.

- **Kept.** The failures of §3.3 look shaped alike: each is a carry across an audience hop.
  This reminded the seat of the sift skill's rule that the writer supplies every sentence, and
  of the audience-reader's design, which returns a belief and never a verdict. Three
  instruments guarding one boundary (the fragment-to-sentence boundary) in three places. Routed
  to §6 as P4.
- **Kept.** "I wrote them for you, I did not write them for the audience of linkedin" reads, on
  a slow re-read, as a sentence about two audiences for one set of words. The lane's whole
  method is audience separation; this is the sentence the concept came from.
- **Kept.** The surface whose owner edits it by hand is a record, not a source (§3.7). This
  reminded the seat of every content system with a human editor beside an agent; it generalises
  past LinkedIn.
- **Kept.** The research report and the lane sit at two altitudes of one subject; neither
  substitutes for the other, and the next person's profile needs both.
- **Discarded, visibly.** A portrait-sitting analogy (the sitter present, the likeness judged by
  the sitter and by strangers): the lane already uses "sitting"; the analogy adds nothing.
- **Discarded, visibly.** The PDF baseline as a photograph: forced.
- **Discarded, visibly.** Skill pinning as keyword strategy: `editorial-guidance.md` already
  holds a keyword section; nothing new.
