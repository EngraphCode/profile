# What the LinkedIn lane taught about editorial corrections, approaches and professional identity

The second retrospective on the LinkedIn lane (26 September to 25 October 2026), run on 28 October
at the owner's word: "Run the full retro now. What did we learn about editorial corrections,
approaches, professional identity and related concepts during the Linkedin work." It extends the
record of 27 October
(`2026-10-27-why-the-linkedin-lane-cost-what-it-cost-and-where-its-lessons-live.md`), which holds
the arc, the cost, the causal stack, the counterfactual and the disposition of every lesson by
home, and repeats none of it. This record answers a different question: what the concepts are.
A lesson stored as a rule is applied by surface analogy on the next lane, which is the oscillation
the causal stack named at its meta layer; a concept with its worked instance is applied by
recognition. The CV chapter is the next lane and reads this first.

Sources, read in full: the owner's typed turns from the three seats (three ledgers in the private
repository, about 290 turns, read in order against what the seat had just done), the nine
editorial decision records, the two editorial directives, the four editorial skills as landed by
pull request 354, the public and private author files, the thread record and the napkin blocks of
the lane. Under the second cut (`privacy.md` §Private editorial material) the record quotes the
owner's words about the writing and the process only; every instance about him is given as a
class.

## 1. What an editorial correction turned out to be

- **A correction is of the model, never of the sentence.** His word on 8 October, when a draft
  echoed his loudest correction back: "the understanding is correct, but the editorial intent and
  execution should not simply align with what I said most vehemently, these words are here to
  achieve an impact". The seat's recurring response to a correction was a string replacement at
  the place it was given: after the identity correction, a class of evidence deleted ("This edit
  takes away too much", 23 October); after "no cuts", nothing moved; after "stop relisting", a
  live question closed on his behalf. The concept: a correction names an axis and a direction;
  the next draft is re-derived from the corrected understanding, and the words are still chosen
  for the reader. Home: `write-for-the-reader` §5 and its last gotcha.
- **Corrections come in three classes, and only one of them costs.** Fact corrections (a date, a
  title, who did what, a product's name) were cheap and expected; the owner said so on 7 October.
  Process corrections (a change made without him, a settled item relisted, ceremony in an
  editorial sitting) became standing agreements the day they were given. Direction corrections,
  of the seat's model of the person or the task, were the fourteen the record of 27 October
  counts, and each returned the seat to a place it had already reached. The lane's finding is
  why: a direction correction was treated as the first class and patched locally, when every
  draft since the error carried it. The response that works is to stop and re-derive the whole
  from the corrected model. Lands in this pull request (proposal 1).
- **The author's own edits are corrections by demonstration, and the strongest evidence of the
  voice.** "I have made edits via the editor, read, reflect, report, stop" recurred through the
  lane; his edits were read for what they taught about the intent and the rhythm, never checked
  for compliance with the method, and an item he called his own edit got no proposal. He asked
  for the learning back in the other direction too ("I enjoy learning about structure and pacing
  in writing like this"). Home: `write-for-the-reader` §5, §8.
- **"Is it good writing?" is a test run apart from context.** "Read all of that for style, forget
  context, is it good writing" (8 October); "a litany of facts with no soul and no connection"
  (21 October). The seat judged copy by coverage and by diffs; the author judged it by reading as
  a person. A diff shows motion and says nothing about reading; copy is judged aloud. Home:
  EDR-009; `write-for-the-reader` §5.
- **One authority, one standard, everything else information.** The authority is the author; the
  standard is editorial excellence (does it read, does it leave the belief); a reader's finding,
  a reviewer's comment, an earlier document, a taxonomy and a limit are information. Three were
  confused in the lane and each produced the generic profile: "just because the readers flicked
  around on them, does that mean they are a problem? what would we lose?"; "that is pandering to a
  reader finding that was never a problem". Home: `write-for-the-reader` §7 and gotchas; the
  firing rule.
- **What is not said is composed, with a reason of the same kinds as what is said.** "What are
  we choosing not to say" (12 October). The lane's omissions each had a reason: a figure that
  spoke to an identity the surface does not express; a jurisdictional detail that "would only
  detract"; a date the author did not want attached; an impact not yet measured ("it is early
  days"); a design that was never built, not described as a build; a fact about a repository's
  future contents. A true fact is not a reason to include it, and an omission is a decision, not a
  default. Lands in this pull request (proposal 2, the general form in §3 of the skill).

## 2. The approaches that held

- **Understanding before expression, and the whole before the parts.** "The first thing we need
  is a model of the impact we seek"; "design the profile as a whole, for a specific effect, not
  one piece at a time, this is a system" (7 October); at the About's success, "we finally got to
  both an understanding and an expression of that understanding". Density is "a global, not a
  local, property" (22 October) and the last read is holistic: the right story, told to the right
  people, in the right way. Homes: `write-for-the-reader` §1, §3, §6; `editorial-strategy.md`.
- **The division of labour is total on both sides.** The seat writes every sentence and the
  author decides every one: "if I was going to write the thing myself then you would not be
  involved" (7 October) and "all the decisions are mine, you still need to make suggestions for
  improvements" (23 October). A finding reaches the author as a proposed sentence, never as a
  question the seat could have answered by drafting. Home: `write-for-the-reader` §5.
- **A discussion before a change; the author's instruments.** "I want a DISCUSSION" (12
  October) after links were traded away to fit a count. The table edited in place, the numbered
  reply, the self-contained question cards, the diff against the last accepted version, the local
  editor he used for every edit of his own. Home: `write-for-the-reader` §5.
- **Evidence before claim, with the author's authority as one kind of evidence.** The Skills list
  was selected, not inherited, and every name was linked to the cards that show it; the top names
  to all their evidence, not one example. A claim about the author's own work stands on the
  author's authority ("the framework is innovative, I invented it"); an accepted fact among the
  readers stands as a fact; a claim the reader would doubt is shown. The opposite failure was the
  seat's invention of plausible facts about the author's world: a description of an
  organisation's data no source gave, a repository said to hold what it does not, an organisation
  said to have one open repository. Three instances; lands in this pull request (proposal 3).
- **The limit is read at the counter and then treated as information.** The response to a field
  that refuses a card is recomposition across the surface's parts (the why, the call, the cost
  and the consequence on the card; the how on the project entries), never a cut and never a
  partition by date. Home: EDR-009; `write-for-the-reader` gotchas; `linkedin-profile`.
- **Names follow their owners, by audience.** The product's name as the organisation markets it;
  vendor terms only as the vendor's listing; an official name in titles and a descriptive name in
  running prose for a general reader; a product name capitalised so it stands as a concept. Home:
  EDR-007; `write-for-the-reader` §6 and gotchas.
- **Copy that ages is a choice to surface.** "The last two and a half years" became "the last few
  years" at the author's hand on 8 October ("then I don't have to update it every month"); a
  present-progressive state dates a card the same way. A profile is read for months. Lands in this
  pull request (proposal 4, extending the tense gotcha).
- **High-impact claims are made once, prominently**, and the organisation's own published figures
  are used where they serve the author (19 October). Home: `write-for-the-reader` §3.
- **The source of truth follows the author's hand.** When the author edits the live surface, the
  repository holds a record of the live state and a read-back precedes every write. Home:
  `write-for-the-reader` §5; `linkedin-profile`.
- **The seat's instruments are built to the smallest shape that reads and changes the text**, and
  the sitting's attention is the author's. Home: `write-for-the-reader` §5; `proportionality`.

## 3. Professional identity on a surface

- **Identity is the belief the whole surface leaves, named by the author in a few words, and it
  is an axis, not a title.** The lane's axis is public in EDR-008 (originates, makes sense of
  problems that have no shape yet, shapes strategy and systems with consequences that outlast the
  author) and in the author file's standing decision (the innovator, never the engineering
  leader). It applies to the whole as much as to each card. A discipline word (scientist) names a
  mindset and an approach, never a position, so it is evidence of the identity and never a title.
  Homes: EDR-008; `editorial-guidance.md` §Conveying a person; `docs/editorial/author.md`.
- **A fact in hand is not yet a signal; the identity decides what it is evidence of.** The lane's
  clearest instance is a decade of test automation on the author's record: for a delivery
  leader's profile it is craft; for this profile it is the way trying new things was made cheap,
  and it stayed as that. A reliability figure spoke to an operations identity and was left out. A
  class of engineering evidence removed after the identity correction came back as evidence of
  range. The same fact serves different identities differently, and a fact that serves no belief
  of this surface is left out however true. Lands in this pull request (proposal 2).
- **Circumstance is not identity.** The seat read the author's current sector as the field, an
  example named in passing as a goal, and the loudest correction as the whole direction, and
  drafted a generic profile from each. Identity comes only from the author's naming of it; a
  circumstantial fact is material, never the frame. Lands in this pull request (proposal 5,
  extending the external-authority gotcha).
- **Level shows and is never argued; titles are facts the author chooses for the story.** A title
  dropped from a tenure because it named a direction the author does not want; another kept
  because it is correct; titles rewritten by discussion with the author and never by the seat.
  An org chart, a reporting line or a budget sentence argues a title up, which is justification.
  Home: EDR-008; `editorial-voice` §What an adjective needs.
- **Authority is stated as possession of a domain inside the purpose it serves.** The lane ended
  on the Principal card's first sentence, three forms compared with the author on 24 and 25
  October: the one that held says what the author owns within the organisation's purpose, in the
  author's voice, and leaves the reporting line to the structured data. "Set" for an author who
  described enabling, guiding and holding space was the opposite of what they said; the verb is
  chosen with the author. Lands in this pull request (proposal 6).
- **Attribution is precise and generous.** What the author conceived, formulated, built and
  steered; what the organisation's experts authored; which rebuild of three the author drove and
  which they only touched; a project that was research and never a build; co-authors restored.
  A claim that survives inspection by the people who were there is the claim a verifier trusts.
  Homes: EDR-007; `write-for-the-reader` §3; `editorial-voice` §Verbs.
- **Range is evidence of the identity, not a departure from it.** Old evidence, side work and
  the "other things" sentence stayed as ballast: endorsements are history ("the past is real, it
  happened"); courses done for fun kept and one found useless dropped; teaching and guiding kept
  as the place the author learned to engage an audience. Home: `editorial-guidance.md` §Breadth
  as range; `linkedin-profile` §The Skills section.
- **For a long tenure at a mission organisation, the card is the organisation's arc told through
  the author's interventions at each scale.** "This is now intended to read as the impactful
  history of Oak and my place in it" (19 October), agreed with the readers: the responsibility,
  the two decisions, the organisation growing and the intervention made at each scale, the choice
  to stay, with the organisation's published figures once and prominently. Home: EDR-009
  decision 2; a candidate shape for the CV, lands in this pull request (proposal 7).
- **Honesty about what is unmeasured, remembered or contested.** An impact "not yet defined or
  measured" is not claimed; an award remembered but not recorded is named as unconfirmed and the
  recorded ones carry the verb; an absolute about a field's history is checked and written "true
  as stated"; an incomplete history supports a bounded claim ("a top two position") and no more.
  Home: `write-for-the-reader` §6.

## 4. Related concepts, with their homes

- Words never cross an audience hop unchanged: the organising concept of the exploration of 25
  October; `write-for-the-reader` §2.
- Every reader decides on one axis; a mis-cast reader's findings are discarded with the reason:
  EDR-008; the `audience-reader` template.
- Readers report beliefs, never inventory; stock-taking is struck in triage: `write-for-the-reader`
  §7.
- Repetition across a surface with many entry points is traced to each surface's intent, never
  smoothed: `write-for-the-reader` §10.
- Surfaces stand alone and are coherent, never the same: the profile is never assumed read beside
  the CV, and the CV will be complementary and never contradict it; the `cv` skill.
- Process and information are different sorts of thing, and information about a person has two
  homes by a second cut: `editorial-guidance.md` §The author's information; `privacy.md`.
- The platform is a set of surfaces with its own shows and hides, verified at the counter and
  proved by re-opening the dialog: `linkedin-profile`.
- An allusion is weighed for the reader it is for, and the author's reason for it is private: a
  borrowed line was weighed against its original on 17 October and settled before moving on; no
  home beyond this record, one instance.

## 6. What the CV chapter inherits

Information, not instruction: the profile as applied is the most verified source of the author's
facts the estate holds (`linkedin/profile.md`, the Skills list with its evidence map, the Oak
figures with their sources); the shape decisions EDR-007 to EDR-009 and the two candidate shapes
for the principal tenure; the public author file and the private one; the coherence check in the
`cv` skill (facts identical, beliefs the same, moments by surface, verbs the same, no copied
sentence, the private boundary); the owner's words of 25 October: the CV "doesn't need to be the
same, it does need to be coherent and complementary and not contradict" the profile.
