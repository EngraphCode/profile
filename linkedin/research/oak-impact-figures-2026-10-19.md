# Oak's published impact figures and their sources

Read 19 October 2026 · Supports the [profile](../profile.md) and the
[profile replacement](../profile-replacement.md)

Oak National Academy publishes an independent evaluation by ImpactEd each year and, as an
arm's-length body, an annual report and accounts on gov.uk. This note records the figures those
documents carry, with their periods and page references, so that the LinkedIn copy can draw on
them without a second search. Every figure is Oak's or ImpactEd's own, quoted as published; the
copy chooses which to use, and the decision container records the choice.

## Definitions and reading notes

- **Teacher counts changed definition in 2024/25.** The 2023/24 figure of 192,760 is Total
  Teacher Users, which adds an estimate of teachers using Oak resources shared offline. The
  2024/25 figure of 196,143 is Direct Teacher Users, teachers who downloaded a resource, used the
  curriculum visualiser ten times or started a lesson in Aila in the last six months. The report
  says the two cannot be compared directly (2024-25 report, page 4, footnote 1).
- **"Nearly three in four" is schools, not teachers.** The 72% is the share of schools in England
  with at least one member of staff who accessed Oak. The share of teachers is "over one in
  three" (2023/24 report, from DfE's workforce count).
- **Pupil use fell as teacher use rose.** Pupil lessons fell 41% in 2024/25 while downloads rose
  128%; the report reads this as the shift from emergency remote learning to classroom planning
  and delivery.
- **Retention is a comparison of intentions**, not a measured effect; the 2024-25 report says it
  "could be linked" to Oak use.
- **The platform rebuild in the annual reports is measured by user migration.** The 2023-24
  report records "rebuilding Oak's platform for both teacher and pupil users" with over half of
  users migrated by March 2024, and the 2024-25 report records the migration complete. Jim's
  word of 19 October 2026: this is the same rebuild the profile describes, defined by migration
  rather than by engineering completeness; the rebuilt platform served Oak's public site within
  six months of the start, then the teacher experience, then the pupil experience.
- **The annual reports quote the evaluations with a lag.** The 2024-25 chair's foreword gives
  "five hours a week" and "one in three teachers" from the 2023/24 evaluation, while the
  2024-25 evaluation gives a median of four hours.
